<ol type="1">

<div align="center">
<img class="img-fluid"  src="./images/Prod_2.png" alt=""><br>           
</div><br/>
<div align="center">
<img class="img-fluid"  src="./images/Prod_2_1.png" alt=""><br> 
<b>Fig. 2. Partial fraction form of continuous transfer function</b><br/>							  
</div><br/>

<li> Click on 'Discretization' dropdown-menu to get different methods.  </li><br/>
<div align="center">
<img class="img-fluid"  src="./images/Prod_3.png" alt=""><br> 
<b>Fig. 3. Dropdown menu button to get different discretization methods</b><br/>
